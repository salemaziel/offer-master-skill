Evaluate and score the commercial offer across the four value equation levers.

Use `skills/offer-levers/SKILL.md`.

Input: $ARGUMENTS

Procedure:
1. **Score the 4 Levers (1 to 10)**:
   - **Result**: How specific and desired is the promised outcome? (1 = vague benefit, 10 = quantified, life/business-altering result with clear benchmark).
   - **Belief**: How confident is the buyer that they will achieve it? (1 = no proof, high skepticism; 10 = case studies, verifiable tracking, proof-backed process, risk reversal).
   - **Speed**: How soon does the buyer experience tangible progress? (1 = months of waiting; 10 = instant or 48-hour first win).
   - **Ease**: How low is the effort and sacrifice required from the buyer? (1 = massive client toil; 10 = turnkey, frictionless, clear step-by-step guidance).
2. **Identify Constraints**:
   - Compare scores against price. Identify the lowest-scoring levers causing hesitation.
3. **Recommend High-Leverage Fixes**:
   - Deliver 2-3 concrete changes (e.g. adding a week-one win to boost Speed, or adding proof/risk reversal to boost Belief).
