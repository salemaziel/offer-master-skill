Format the synthesized decisions from all stages into a concise, paste-ready offer card.

Use `skills/offer-card/SKILL.md`.

Input: $ARGUMENTS

Procedure:
1. **Offer Card Formatting**:
   - **Name**: Sticky name that passed the text test.
   - **One-Line Promise**: Clear target audience, measurable outcome, and timeframe.
   - **Week-One Win**: First tangible deliverable received in 7 days.
   - **Inclusions**: Bulleted obstacle-based deliverables.
   - **Bonuses**: 1-2 focused doubt killers.
   - **Guarantee**: Fair, capped guarantee with exact conditions.
   - **Urgency**: Real deadline or operational constraint.
   - **Price**: Exact price and payment plan terms.
2. **Number Labeling**:
   - Every single figure must be explicitly marked as `(yours)` or `(estimate)`.
3. **Verification Checklist**:
   - 3-5 item pre-launch checklist covering proof validation, terms review, and tracking setup.
4. **Next-Month Test**:
   - One actionable, low-risk test to execute over the next 30 days to measure conversion lift.
